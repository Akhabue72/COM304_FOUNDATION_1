# Council Proposal 3

## Explanation of this proposal
A VPN (virtyal Private Network) is a system that makes a tunnel for your internet traffic to pass through while hiding your IP address by routing your data through a remote server. This is done to protect your activity online from advertisers and your ISP (Internet Service Provider).

## Questions to ask about the proposal

* If a councilor's home network gets breached, who is legally and financially responsible?
* What happens when some counilors are unable to connect due to a weak internet connection?
* What are the full costs of keeping the vpn active 24/7?

## Evaluation of the proposal
A vpn is essential for modern council operations but it introduces the problem of accountibility with security breaches. For it to succeed, it needs to be treated as a governance and compliance project first, and a technology project second: The council mus invest in both the policy and technology.
